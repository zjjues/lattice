# 固定翼发动机故障处置预案

Tags: flight, failure, engine_failure, glide

1. Confirm the airspeed trend and engine telemetry from independent signals.
2. Maintain the aircraft's documented best-glide airspeed and avoid abrupt turns.
3. Select a reachable recovery area and keep the operator in the decision loop.
4. Escalate only after checking vehicle-specific limits and current environment.

This is a demonstration SOP, not an operational flight instruction.
